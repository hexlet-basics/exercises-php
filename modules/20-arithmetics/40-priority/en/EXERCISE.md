You and 4 friends (5 people in total) have ordered 2 pizzas for 300 rubles each and 5 drinks for 40 rubles each. You need to split the bill equally.

Write a single-line program with `print_r()`, placing the parentheses so that the total amount is calculated first, and then divided among everyone:

```text
2 * 300 + 5 * 40 / 5  =  640  ← without parentheses the division applies only to the drinks
```

Expected output: `160`.
